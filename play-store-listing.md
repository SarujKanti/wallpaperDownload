## Full description (max 4000)

Wallvyn – Wallpapers for every mood.

Find the perfect background for your phone in seconds. Wallvyn brings you HD and 4K wallpapers sorted into easy categories, with a clean, ad-free design.

🎨 CATEGORIES
• Trending – popular wallpapers in a fresh order every day
• Latest – newly added wallpapers
• Nature
• Space
• Cars
• City
• Abstract
• Minimal
• Dark
• Liked – your saved favourites in one place

Each category shows only wallpapers on that subject.

🔍 SEARCH
Search any keyword, like mountains, ocean, flowers or sunset, and get matching wallpapers and photos.

📱 HD & 4K QUALITY
Category wallpapers are HD or higher, and many are 4K. Every card shows its quality (from HD and Full HD up to 2K, 4K and higher) and orientation before you open it.

✨ ONE TAP TO SET
• Full-screen preview before you apply
• Set as home screen, lock screen or both
• Download the full-resolution image to Pictures/Wallvyn/Download
• Share any wallpaper with friends
• Tap ♥ to keep favourites in your Liked tab

🌓 LIGHT & DARK THEME
Choose Light, Dark or follow your phone's setting.

🔔 STAY UPDATED
Get a daily reminder when new trending wallpapers arrive. You can turn notifications off at any time.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• No account or sign-up
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your liked wallpapers and settings stay on your device

Works on phones and tablets.

Wallpapers come from Wallhaven, Pixabay and the Wallvyn collection. All images belong to their creators. If you own an image and want it credited or removed, use "Report or request removal" in the app menu or email 14skd12@gmail.com.
